Urban Terrain
Norwich Central

Norwich Central explores the terrain structure of one of England's most historic urban landscapes. Beneath the modern street network lies a subtle but expressive topography shaped by river valleys, historic settlement patterns and centuries of development.


This terrain study uses Environment Agency LiDAR data processed through the Oddscape Terrain Engine™ to reveal elevation, landform and surface relationships that are often hidden within the built environment. Hillshade and Relative Elevation Model analysis expose the underlying landscape framework of the city.


The study highlights the relationship between the River Wensum, surrounding valley slopes and the elevated ground that influenced the historic growth of Norwich. Features that appear subtle at street level become far more apparent when viewed through terrain visualisation and three-dimensional modelling.


Designed for exploration through augmented reality and interactive terrain models, the project demonstrates how LiDAR-derived terrain data can support urban landscape interpretation, heritage understanding, education and spatial analysis.
