Coastal Heritage Landscape
Skara Brae Terrain Study

Situated on the western coast of Mainland Orkney archipelago, Skara Brae is one of Europe's best-preserved Neolithic settlements and forms part of the Heart of Neolithic Orkney UNESCO World Heritage Site.


This terrain study explores the relationship between Skara Brae, Skaill Bay, the surrounding coastal platform and the wider landscape of the Orkney archipelago.


Skara Brae Terrain Study Neolithic settlement, coastal cliffs and archaeological landscape around Skaill Bay, Orkney. Derived from 50 cm DTM and 2023 LiDAR datasets using the Oddscape Terrain Engine.
