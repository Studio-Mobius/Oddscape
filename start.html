Start a Project

Tell me where the site is, what you need to understand, and how the terrain study will be used. I can then advise whether the available data is suitable and what level of visualisation is appropriate.

Useful starting details

You do not need a technical brief to begin. A location, a rough boundary and a short description of what you are trying to show is enough to start the conversation.

  • Site name or nearest place.
  • Approximate boundary, postcode, grid reference or map link.
  • Whether the site is coastal, upland, river, heritage or development related.
  • What you need the terrain study for: presentation, interpretation, planning, design, teaching or exploration.
  • Whether you need still images, AR preview, or production mesh export.

What happens next

I will check the likely data availability, scale of the site and level of processing required. If the project is suitable, I will confirm the most appropriate commission type and provide a clear quote before work begins.

Before you send

Production mesh exports are quoted separately. The AR model is a preview terrain visualisation and is not a substitute for a cleaned production model unless this is specifically commissioned.

Send your project details and any map links, screenshots or site notes.
