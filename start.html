Start a Project
Every landscape begins with a location and a question. Provide the site details, project objectives, and intended use, and Oddscape will assess the available terrain data and recommend the most appropriate terrain visualisation approach.

What to Send

A fully technical brief is not required at the enquiry stage. Send the essentials and Oddscape can advise what level of terrain visualisation is suitable.

Site Location

Postcode, grid reference, map link, site name, screenshot or rough boundary.

Project Type

Landscape, heritage, coastal, river, upland, estate, development or teaching context.

Outputs Required

Still images, hillshade, REM, AR preview, OBJ, GLB, USDZ or production mesh export.

What Happens Next

Oddscape will check the likely data availability, scale of the site and level of processing required. If the project is suitable, the most appropriate commission type and a clear quote will be confirmed before work begins.

Before You Send

The AR model is a preview terrain visualisation. Production mesh exports are prepared separately and quoted according to area, resolution, cleanup and delivery format.


For larger sites, bathymetry, heritage interpretation or detailed production models, a custom quote may be required.
