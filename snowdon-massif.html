Snowdon Massif Terrain Study
Snowdon Massif Terrain Study v1.0. Terrain visualisation generated using the Oddscape Terrain Engine™ from Welsh Government LiDAR DTM data.
A high-resolution upland terrain study of the Snowdon massif, including Llyn Llydaw, Glaslyn, Crib Goch, Garnedd Ugain and the upper slopes of Yr Wyddfa.

Llyn Llydaw and the Snowdon Massif

This study explores one of Wales’ most dramatic mountain landscapes through terrain modelling, hillshade interpretation and interactive AR visualisation. The model captures the relationship between lake basins, glacial landforms, ridge systems and steep upland slopes.

Unlike coastal or lowland studies, the Snowdon massif gives the Oddscape workflow a strongly vertical landscape to interpret, revealing cirques, arêtes, tarn basins and the sculptural structure of the mountain terrain.

Landscape Context

The study area includes Llyn Llydaw, Glaslyn, Crib Goch, Garnedd Ugain and the upper slopes of Yr Wyddfa, forming a compact but highly legible mountain terrain model.

Data Source

Generated from Welsh Government LiDAR DTM data. DTM data represents bare-earth terrain, removing vegetation and surface features to reveal the underlying landform.

Oddscape Outputs

Terrain preview, hillshade, REM analysis, AR terrain model, OBJ and USDZ exports can be generated for interpretation, presentation and interactive viewing.

Use Potential

Suitable for upland landscape interpretation, education, mountain route context, heritage landscape study, environmental analysis and interactive public engagement.

AR Terrain Model

The AR model is a preview terrain visualisation. Production mesh exports are prepared separately and quoted according to area, resolution, cleanup and delivery format.
