Frequently Asked Questions

Practical information about Oddscape commissions, formats, licensing, 3D printing, accuracy and contact.

Can I commission a terrain model?

Yes. Studio Oddscape accepts selected commissions for digital terrain models, landscape visualisations and interpretative studies.

What formats are available?

Outputs depend on the project. They may include still images, mapped datasets, GLB and USDZ interactive models, STL files prepared for 3D printing, and publication-ready material.

Can a model be 3D printed?

Yes. Terrain can be prepared as a watertight printable model. Scale, footprint, vertical treatment and the practical limits of the chosen printing process are agreed for each commission.

Can the work be licensed commercially?

Yes. Commercial, educational, heritage, exhibition and research uses can be licensed on a project basis. Redistribution and reuse are not included unless they are expressly agreed.

How accurate are the models?

Accuracy depends on the resolution, date and quality of the source data. Oddscape records the source and relevant limitations where appropriate. The models are not replacements for legal, boundary or engineering surveys.

Can any location be modelled?

Most locations can be considered, provided suitable elevation data exists or can be obtained. Coverage and achievable detail vary considerably between places.

What does a commission cost?

Cost depends on area, source preparation, level of interpretation, output formats and licensing. A quotation is provided after the project and its intended use have been discussed.

Where can I read about the process?

The Methodology explains how Oddscape approaches data, interpretation, colour, light, accuracy and landscape relationships.

How do I get in touch?

Use the Contact page to discuss a commission, collaboration or licensing opportunity with Studio Oddscape.
