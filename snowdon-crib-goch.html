Snowdon & Crib Goch
Snowdon & Crib Goch Terrain Study. Welsh Government 1m LiDAR DTM interpreted through the Oddscape Terrain Engine™.
A terrain study of the Snowdon massif, centred on Crib Goch, Llyn Llydaw, Glaslyn and the upper slopes of Yr Wyddfa.

Llyn Llydaw and the Mountain Ridge

Crib Goch forms one of Britain’s most recognisable mountain ridges. This study explores the relationship between ridge, lake and valley across the eastern flank of Yr Wyddfa.

The hillshade reveals the underlying shape of the landscape: steep glacial basins, sharp arêtes, upland slopes and the dark linear presence of Llyn Llydaw.

Landscape

A compact upland study area containing Crib Goch, Llyn Llydaw, Glaslyn and surrounding mountain landforms.

Terrain Source

Welsh Government 1m LiDAR DTM, showing the bare-earth structure of the mountain landscape.

Outputs

Hillshade visualisation, terrain preview, colour relief model and Apple Quick Look AR terrain model.

Use

Suitable for landscape interpretation, upland studies, visitor engagement, education and digital terrain presentation.

AR Terrain Model

Explore the Snowdon and Crib Goch terrain model as an interactive tabletop landscape using Apple Quick Look AR.

The AR model is a preview terrain visualisation. Production mesh exports are prepared separately and quoted according to area, resolution, cleanup and delivery format.

Open AR Model

For iPhone and iPad users, the USDZ file should open in Apple Quick Look.

Technical Note

The model was generated from a 1m DTM covering approximately 5 km × 3 km of upland terrain. The cleaned terrain passed Oddscape QA with no detected void cells or spike artefacts.

Terrain source: Welsh Government LiDAR DTM, 1m resolution.
Visualisation and model generation: Oddscape Terrain Engine™ Professional.
