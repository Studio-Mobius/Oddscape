Snowdon & Crib Goch
Snowdon & Crib Goch Terrain Study. Hillshade terrain visualisation generated using the Oddscape Terrain Engine™ from Welsh Government 1m LiDAR DTM data.
A high-resolution upland terrain study of the Snowdon massif, including Crib Goch, Llyn Llydaw, Glaslyn, Garnedd Ugain and the upper slopes of Yr Wyddfa.

Llyn Llydaw and the Snowdon Massif

Crib Goch is one of the most recognisable mountain ridges in Britain. This study uses bare-earth LiDAR terrain data to reveal the relationship between ridge, lake basin, glacial form and upland slope.

Unlike coastal or lowland terrain studies, the Snowdon massif gives the Oddscape workflow a strongly vertical landscape to interpret. The resulting model exposes cirques, arêtes, tarn basins, steep escarpments and the sculptural structure of the mountain terrain.

Study Area 5 km × 3 km

Landscape Context

The study captures a compact but highly legible section of the Snowdon massif, with Llyn Llydaw acting as a visual anchor and Crib Goch forming a sharp ridge line across the upland terrain.

Data Source

Generated from Welsh Government 1m LiDAR DTM data. DTM data represents bare-earth terrain, removing vegetation and surface clutter to reveal the underlying landform.

Oddscape Outputs

The engine produces terrain previews, hillshade interpretation, REM analysis, AR terrain models, OBJ mesh export and client-ready QA documentation.

Use Potential

Suitable for upland landscape interpretation, education, mountain route context, heritage landscape study, environmental analysis and interactive public engagement.

Terrain Outputs

The hillshade output reveals the physical structure of the Snowdon massif, while the colour relief model provides an additional elevation-led interpretation of the same terrain.

AR Terrain Model

The AR model is a preview terrain visualisation. Production mesh exports are prepared separately and quoted according to area, resolution, cleanup and delivery format.

Open AR Model

For iPhone and iPad users, the USDZ file should open in Apple Quick Look for object and AR viewing.

Technical Summary

The Snowdon and Crib Goch terrain model passed Oddscape QA with no detected void cells and no detected spike artefacts. The model was exported from a 1m DTM raster covering approximately 5 km by 3 km of upland terrain.

The study demonstrates the use of the Oddscape Terrain Engine™ for mountain landscapes, expanding the archive beyond coastal, estuarine, urban and heritage terrain studies.

Terrain source: Welsh Government LiDAR DTM, 1m resolution.
Visualisation and model generation: Oddscape Terrain Engine Professional v2.17.
