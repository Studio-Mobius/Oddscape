Studio Work

Terrain-derived prints, cartographic compositions and visual studies developed from LiDAR, elevation data, bathymetry and landscape interpretation.

Terrain as Image

Studio Oddscape produces visual works that sit between mapping, landscape architecture, data interpretation and fine art. These pieces are generated from real terrain, then composed as atmospheric studies of place, form, erosion, memory and movement.

Prints are available as open editions unless otherwise stated. Limited editions, framed works and bespoke site-based commissions can be discussed by email.
