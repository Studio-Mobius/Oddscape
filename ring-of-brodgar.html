Oddscape Place Report OPR-001
Ness of Brodgar
The Ceremonial Basin

The Ness of Brodgar occupies one of the most remarkable ceremonial landscapes in Britain: a narrow isthmus between the Loch of Harray and the Loch of Stenness, surrounded by water, low relief and long horizons.

This Oddscape study reads Brodgar not simply as a stone circle, but as part of a wider landscape system shaped by geology, water, movement, visibility and enduring human attention.

Produced through the Oddscape workflow — OddTerrain for terrain interpretation, Odd3D for immersive model delivery, and OddPress for publication output — this page brings together the terrain model, AR preview and first Oddscape Place Report.
