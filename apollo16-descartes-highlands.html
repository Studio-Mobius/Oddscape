Measured lunar terrain

Apollo 16 was the only crewed lunar landing devoted principally to the ancient lunar highlands. The Descartes landscape revealed a heavily cratered terrain fundamentally different from the mare plains explored earlier.

The terrain is reconstructed from NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Narrow Angle Camera elevation data. The landing location is placed at the centre of the working tile and marked by the Luna Landings gold circle pin. The integrated Oddscape navigation mark identifies lunar north.
