Apollo // 1969–1972

Six landings. Six lunar landscapes.

Each mission encountered a different part of the Moon: mare plains, ejecta deposits, highlands, mountain fronts, valleys and volcanic terrain. The landing-site collection will reconstruct those places as measurable terrain rather than isolated map points.

Mare Tranquillitatis

Tranquility Base
0.674° N · 23.473° E

Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin made humanity's first landing on another world here, on the basaltic plains of the Sea of Tranquility. A 2 m LROC NAC terrain tile is now in production for this site.

Tile 001 // Apollo 11

Tranquility Base

The first detailed landing-site terrain tile will focus on the landscape surrounding Apollo 11's landing point in Mare Tranquillitatis.

Tile 001 · Mare Tranquillitatis

Tranquility Base in measured terrain

The first Luna Landings tile is now being built from a NASA LRO/LROC Narrow Angle Camera digital terrain model covering the Apollo 11 landing region.

The working tile measures approximately 4 × 6 km at a native 2 m terrain grid, with registered LROC orthophotography prepared on the same raster geometry. The Apollo 11 landing point lies within the tile at approximately 0.674° N, 23.473° E.

This source terrain is retained as the geospatial master. A lighter interactive version will be produced for OddView™, GLB, USDZ and augmented-reality delivery.
