Antarctica — Beneath the Ice

Antarctica conceals a vast landscape beneath its ice sheet — mountain ranges, deep basins and continental-scale landforms revealed through decades of geophysical survey.


Studio Oddscape applies its terrain methodology to Bedmap3, translating polar elevation data into terrain relief, analytical imagery and interactive 3D outputs that make the landscape beneath the ice easier to see, interrogate and communicate.


The study explores the relationship between surface, ice and underlying bed topography — from 2D terrain interpretation on screen to an interactive Antarctic landscape in the browser and on a mobile device.

The same Bedmap3 terrain can be read in different ways. Hillshade reveals continental form and relief; contours expose structure, elevation relationships and the geometry of the landscape beneath the ice.
