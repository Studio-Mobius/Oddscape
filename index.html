The Oddscape Terrain Engine™

A terrain visualisation system developed by Studio Oddscape. Built on geospatial workflows, LiDAR data, and landscape-led interpretation, the engine transforms elevation data into clear visual and spatial understanding across coastlines, uplands, estates, heritage sites, and large-scale environments.

  • 3D terrain models, visual studies, and AR-ready landscape experiences
  • Estuaries, coastlines, uplands, mountains, heritage landscapes, and estate-scale sites
  • Complex terrain data translated into accessible visual and spatial understanding

About

Studio Oddscape works with terrain as both data and experience — interpreting landform, elevation, drainage, structure, and movement through LiDAR-derived visualisation and immersive spatial models.

The work supports design, interpretation, planning, education, heritage engagement, and land management by making landscape form easier to see, explore, and communicate.
